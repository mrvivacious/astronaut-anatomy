## Projects Page draft
Want: make it easier to turn the passion for astronomy into a real lived experience.
Idea: a list of projects and experiences that a reader could perform.

## easy ideas
📺 like space, don't know where to start -> watch documentaries and read articles online

🔭 join a telescope or astrophotography group. attend sessions.

🔭 find the nearest observatory and visit

🪐 find the nearest space museum and visit

👽 visit NASA and take a tour

## hands on ideas
🌎 build a diorama of the solar system

🚀 build a model rocket, launch the rocket

🛠️ build a cubesat. launch the cubesat.

## academic
🧑‍🎓 start a space club in your school. invite a guest speaker who has experience with astronomy

🧑‍🏫 Identify what professors in your area have ongoing astronomy research. email them, ask to help

🧑‍🚀 Apply to NASA/spacex internships
